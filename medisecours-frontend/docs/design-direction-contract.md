# Direction contract — MediSecours rebrand (dev-only)

Direction: « Protocole mural » — decided with the owner (impeccable `concept-seed`
binary unavailable in this environment; direction cards were presented manually
and the owner locked this one).

## THESIS

The interface is a first-aid protocol sheet hung on a wall, not a SaaS dashboard.
It answers "what do I do now?" through instruction bands and numbered steps —
never through decorative eyebrow kickers. It refuses the healthcare-SaaS default:
stock hero photo, pastel icon cards, tracked uppercase kickers, blue-gradient
split-auth.

## OWN-WORLD

Paper-sand ground `#F6F3EC` with white "sheet" surfaces at hard edges (radius
0–4px). Navy `#1E3A5F` ink for all text and full instruction bands. Mint
`#10B981` means action/do/ok. Red `#EF4444` is reserved for vital warnings and
SOS only. Display voice: Archivo 600–800 (poster/consign voice, self-hosted).
Body voice: Source Sans 3 400–700 (public-health document voice, self-hosted).
Navy 1px/2px rules; no glass decoration; shadows are soft navy with offset;
radius full only for avatars and status badges.

## STORY

A visitor arrives in a real situation → reads at a glance what to do (red band =
immediate danger, mint = action, numbered steps) → follows the chain → lands on
the right structure or doctor.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

Home opens on a full-width situation band (no generic stock photo): navy/sand
aplat, protocol pictogram, mass Archivo headline, red emergency action + 119,
then the three real situations → three actions visible at the fold.
